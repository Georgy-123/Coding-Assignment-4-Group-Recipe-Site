Edamame

Recipe by: Sydney Ellis

Ingredients

  • Edamame
  • Salt
  • Pepper
  • Paprika
  • Lemon
  • Water

Rate This Recipe

How difficult is this recipe? Easy Intermediate Hard

=

Directions

  1. Rinse Edamame pea pods in strainer
  2. Dice your lemon in fours and set to the side for later
  3. Add water to your pot
  4. Place pot of water on stove and turn it to medium heat and wait for it to boil
  5. Once boiling, add in your Edamame
  6. Let Edamame boil until tender
  7. Once tender to your liking, strain Edamame and let cool for 3 minutes
  8. Place in bowl
  9. Add seasonings
  10. Place bowl lid on and gentle shake until fully covered
  11. Open lid and squeeze desired amount of lemon over seasoned Edamame pea pods
  12. Optional:Place Edamame in a seperate bowl
  13. Enjoy!
